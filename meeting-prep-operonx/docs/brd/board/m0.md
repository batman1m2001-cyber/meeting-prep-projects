## Goal

Meeting Prep on OperonX turns a lead's incoming email into a researched sales brief and sends it to sales for human approval. Approving the brief sends it to sales and saves it to the knowledge base. The flow is built as nested OperonX graphs, one per box of the course brief, and only the web-research step uses LLM agents.

- Criteria:

  - exits end together: one `[a, b, c] >> END`, not a line each
